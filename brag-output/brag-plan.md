# /brag — Tick

**What it is.** A prediction market that settles every 30 seconds, built to be played with your group:
start a room, everyone calls the same window, a live scoreboard re-sorts, and the winner gets a card drawn
from their real on-chain record to post.

**Angle.** Not "a faster market". The brag is the *group*: the bet is small, the clock is short, and the
prize is proof you can show your friends. Lead with the clock, end on the card.

**Hook (0–2.5s).** A black frame, one line: *Thirty seconds.* The room clock ticking down behind it. If you
read nothing else, you know the speed.

**Tone.** Polished, punchy. Six scenes, hard cuts on the beat, nothing on screen that is not the real app.

**Identity.** Near-black #0b0d08, acid lime #d8f35f, Geist. The phone is the hero; type sits beside it.

**Storyboard** (~21s, 1920×1080, 30fps)

| # | Time | On screen | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.6 | Room clock counting down, tight | Thirty seconds. |
| 2 | 2.6–6.4 | Trade screen, chart live, Above / Below | Call it. Above or below. |
| 3 | 6.4–10.6 | Room scoreboard re-sorting | Your whole group, same window. |
| 4 | 10.6–14.4 | The card, lime glow | Win, and the proof is on chain. |
| 5 | 14.4–17.8 | Feed, card in a post | Post it. Let them see. |
| 6 | 17.8–21.0 | Logo, tagline, URL | Compete in groups. Flex your profits. |

**Sound.** One piece: a low drone in A with a soft pad, and a tick on each cut, because the product is
called Tick. A short riser into the card reveal. Effects sit under the music, nothing spiky.

**Share line.** "Thirty-second markets you play with your group. Win, and the card proving it is on chain."
