# Demo voiceover — record this if the synthetic read sounds off

The cut is 61 seconds. Each line starts when its scene does, with a short beat of picture first. Read it
flat and unhurried, the way you would explain it to someone sitting next to you; no sell, no lift at the
end of sentences. Pause where the blank lines are.

| # | Starts | Scene | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Landing | Prediction markets are slow. The quickest one you can name takes five minutes to settle. Tick settles in thirty seconds. |
| 2 | 0:08 | Feed | And it doesn't open on a trading screen. It opens on a feed, where the thing people post is their profit. |
| 3 | 0:15 | Markets | Every market is one question. Will this price be higher, thirty seconds from now. |
| 4 | 0:24 | Trade | You get the live chart, the strike, and two sides. Ten cents is enough to take one. It settles on Pyth, on chain. Nobody referees it. |
| 5 | 0:34 | Groups | The part that makes it stick is the group. Rooms, chat, and a leaderboard, all of it on chain. |
| 6 | 0:42 | Room | The owner picks a market. Everyone calls the same thirty seconds, and the table re-sorts as each window settles. |
| 7 | 0:50 | Card | Win, and Tick draws your card from your real record. Verified on chain, so the number can't be faked. |
| 8 | 0:57 | Close | Thirty second markets, for people who'd rather trade with their friends. Tick. |

Record it as one take, in a quiet room, phone held a hand's width away and slightly off to the side so
you aren't breathing into it. Send me the file and I'll line it up against the picture and remix.

To rebuild the video with a new voice track once the lines are in `media/voice/out/*.wav`:
`cd media/scripts && node build-demo.mjs`
