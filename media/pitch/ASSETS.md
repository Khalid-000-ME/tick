# Pitch video assets

Everything here is real footage of the running product, recorded at phone size (430x932), and real
screenshots. Nothing is a mockup. Use these with `submission/pitch-video-brief.md`.

## Full demo

`00-full-demo.mp4` — 70s, the whole product tour with voiceover, ending on the Monad evidence card.
Cut from this if you want continuity; use the clips below if you want silent B-roll under a voice.

## Clips, silent, for cutting under the script

| File | Length | Shows | Use at |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clips/01-room.mp4` | 14s | a room: the 30s window, the chart, the crowd split, Above/Below | 0:15–0:45, the core beat |
| `clips/02-trade.mp4` | 13s | the trading terminal, window picker, strike, pool sentiment | 0:45–1:10, why thirty seconds |
| `clips/03-card.mp4` | 7s | a results card, verified on chain, with the share actions | 0:15–0:45, the payoff |
| `clips/04-feed.mp4` | 10s | the feed: cards posted by real wallets | 1:10–1:35, early signal |
| `clips/05-groups.mp4` | 16s | groups and the chat where cards land | 0:15–0:45 or 1:10–1:35 |
| `clips/06-markets.mp4` | 14s | the markets list, crypto and sport, all window lengths | 0:45–1:10 |
| `clips/07-landing.mp4` | 11s | the landing page and the scroll story | opening, if you want a cold open |

Each clip has a visible cursor moving and tapping, so it reads as someone using the app rather than
a screen recording of nothing happening.

## The one to put on screen when you talk about Monad

`stills/07-monad-proof.png`. Every number on it was read live from the chain and the indexer when it
was rendered: chain 10143, the Tick contract, the head block, the block the indexer has reached, and
a real settled window with pools of $1.00 above and $3.00 below, settled 11 seconds after close,
paying a $0.01 bounty to the wallet that settled it.
